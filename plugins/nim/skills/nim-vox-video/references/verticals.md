# Vertical presets

Shared core for every vertical: one paper desk, motion on twos, engine-drawn text, rim + shadow
on every cutout, one accent colour with fixed meaning, events on hook words, one active element.

## Tech (devices, software, AI, hardware hacks)
- **Script**: open on the most surprising concrete fact (price, date, number). Short declaratives,
  slight irony allowed. End on a dry punch line ("Nobody asked.") or what comes next.
- **Palette**: graph paper `#faf7ee`, ink `#16161a`, accent electric blue `#1f6feb` (= new / on),
  accent2 yellow `#ffd23f` (= price / numbers / punch).
- **Hero**: the real device from a real photo, kept for the whole video. Its screen is a `screen`
  layer: show UI, code, games, sequencers there (code-drawn or Seedance insert).
- **Beat recipes**: price slam + circle; exploded view / lift reveal (generate the inside with
  NB Pro Edit + reference); grid of N copies with recoloured screens + counter; laptop typing
  code; comparison side-by-side (drop the rival object next to the hero); push-in to the screen
  for "it runs X".
- **Seedance**: only for screen footage (gameplay, UI scrolling, a hand pressing keys).
- **Avoid**: generic gears/circuit clip-art, fake UI text from generators, real third-party logos.

## Product / brand ads (6-30 s)
- **Script**: claim → proof → CTA. Every claim sourced; the playbook decides wording.
- **Palette**: brand colours mapped to accent / accent2; paper stays neutral.
- **Hero**: product from the client's photos (cutout). Feature callouts as strips + arrows on
  the word. Price/offer as a `slam` strip. End card: logo from the client file as `image` with
  `paper: false`, CTA strip, small disclaimer `text`.
- **Seedance**: optional 1 insert of the product in use (hands, pour, wear), 480-720p.
- **Avoid**: generated packaging text, competitor logos, more than 3 claims in 15 s.

## Social / creator (hooks, trends, reactions)
- **Script**: hook in the first 1.5 s (question or shocking number), fast 2 s beats, meme tone.
- **Palette**: bright paper, ink, accent `#ff4d6d`, accent2 `#ffd23f`. 9:16 by default.
- **Devices**: zoom punches (camera key `dur` 0.15), slam words, post/comment cards drawn in code
  (`strip`/`text` on a white card), emoji-free stickers, reaction cutouts (non-real people only,
  or supplied photos).
- **Avoid**: documentary seriousness, censor bars, long holds.

## Finance / money
- **Script**: number → consequence → who pays. Precise figures with source.
- **Palette**: cream, ink, green `#0f9d58` (up), red `#d64545` (down) used only for direction.
- **Devices**: counters, annotated line drawn as `marker path`, grid of scale (N icons, few
  highlighted), banknote/coin cutouts, document strips with a highlighted line.
- **Avoid**: investment advice framing, stock-ticker clip-art.

## Doc / history / crime
- **Script**: Fern DNA: date + place + small action cold open, calm voice, tension from facts,
  final line ≤ 12 words cliffhanger (unresolved object, dated jump, missing piece, quiet
  contradiction, price line).
- **Palette**: warm newsprint, ink, forest green `#23824f`, mustard `#e8b931`.
- **Devices**: document strips (typewriter font) with highlighter sweep, route map as `marker
  path` drawn over a generated map texture, timeline of dated strips, evidence board (cutouts +
  `marker path` string), archival photo cutouts (real archive where possible).
- **Seedance**: rare atmosphere insert (rain on a window, a moving train) inside a photo frame.
- **Avoid**: gore, invented quotes, generated "archival" photos of real events presented as real.

## Motion density by length
| length | beats | events per beat | camera moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6-10 s | 3-4 | 2-3 | 1-2 |
| 15 s | 6-7 | 2-4 | 4-5 |
| 30 s | 12-14 | 2-3 | 6-8 |
| 60-90 s | 25-40 | 2-3 | 1 per 2 beats, add a full-screen insert every ~30 s |
